What this can and cannot show

  • The referee posts only the top ~25 scores and top ~10 positions each sweep. Full balances live in sweep files that are not public, so a complete ranking of all owners cannot be recomputed from the rooms today.
  • Rooms are a ring buffer. Very old posts may already be gone.
  • The referee marks published scores at its global price (the volume-weighted price of the contest's own settled trades, rule 14), not at the Hyperliquid reference. Final scores use S, the last xyz:NVDA trade on Hyperliquid before 10:00 UTC on 4 October 2026, so a key's final score moves by its position × (S − global).
